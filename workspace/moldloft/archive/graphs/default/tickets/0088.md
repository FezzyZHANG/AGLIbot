The JSONL media map uses a lock per Store instance and one fixed temporary name. Two processes updating different images can lose a row or fail replacement. #86 covers ownership of concurrent fields on the same image.

Acceptance
Use two independent writers and distinct image hashes. Interleave updates and restart both readers. Both rows and owner edits survive, the published map remains readable, and a retry cannot revert the other writer.
