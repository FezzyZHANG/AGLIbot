The owner accepted audit finding A05. Fix both independent causes: (1) an Offer captured before a slow model call can be sent after the owner disables/deletes the sticker or narrows its scope; (2) whole-record stale sighting/sent-count/recaption writes can restore Active/Any or resurrect a deleted record after a successful owner edit.

Define the revocation boundary at final dispatch and recheck current status/scope/chat/file. Make metadata updates field-specific or version-checked so counters, sightings and recaption cannot undo owner-managed status, scope or text. Account for bot and web being separate processes; a per-instance Mutex or Mongo atomic replacement alone is insufficient.

Acceptance: pause a synthetic model after selection, then disable/narrow/delete before dispatch; prohibited images must not be sent while ordinary reply text remains deliverable. Deterministically interleave owner edits/deletes with sightings, counters and recaption; owner decisions survive, stale upserts do not resurrect records, counts remain correct. Include the supported JSONL and MongoDB boundaries.
