Accepted decision n-agli-dec-groups is not implemented: on_message still drops messages from groups outside config/social.yaml `groups`. Found by Astra while implementing #6.

Per the decision: every joined group is read-only by default; its messages are stored under that group's namespace and scope and feed nightly reflect, memory, person cards and #6's learned group cards. Read-only groups never reach floor, triage or speak: no per-message model calls, no posts, not even when @-mentioned. social.yaml `groups` stays the reply allowlist. A group can be marked ignored (not stored at all).

Reflect over read-only groups gets its own per-night token/cost budget so a noisy group cannot crowd out the owner's chats; past the budget, sample messages instead of reading all, and log it.

Done when: a message from a non-allowlisted group is stored with the group's ns/scope and makes no model call; an ignored group is not stored; nightly reflection over read-only groups stops at its budget and samples; allowlisted groups behave as today.
