Deferred by design: auto-resume after crash (tasks currently return to inbox), dedupe repeated messages/tool calls, and file/DB consistency repair.

Owner priority clarification (2026-11-01): the main difference from dsh-agent-teams is concurrency. AGLI currently has one active delegated job; the planned background-slot feature (#37) defaults max_jobs to 1. Keep this backlog and its new idempotency children low priority. Revisit when repeated work is observed, or overlapping attempts, automatic recovery or multiple workers become actual usage. Retain the cheap verify-before-resume step in #35 without importing the plugin's whole scheduler.

Context update (2026-11-01, after #25): DSH is now AGLI's primary programmable hand, and dsh-agent-teams is itself a DSH plugin. That makes its mechanisms easier to borrow, not a reason to adopt its multi-member runtime; concurrency still arrives only through #37 or a later owner decision.

The new children cover attempt/result deduplication and follow-up receipt handling. Defensive review gates, review-to-artifact hash binding and additional review loops are outside this batch; their expected benefit and priority are low at the present workload.
