A10: a lost forward-send acknowledgement causes an ordinary chunk fallback that can duplicate a delivered message. A11: queueing is outside the response timeout, reconnect can dispatch expired requests, and unacknowledged pending entries outlive their caller. The original native feature milestone is #70/#76.

Acceptance
Model queued, written, acknowledged, definitively refused and unknown outcomes. A dropped acknowledgement must produce no automatic second send. Preserve safe fallback after definitive refusal. Check expired requests before write on reconnect, bound a full queue, and observe pending entries reclaimed after caller timeout on a healthy socket.
