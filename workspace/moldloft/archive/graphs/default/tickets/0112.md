Owner sign-off 2026-11-23: restore lessons and Widen together. At 590a346 only solo thinking and the journal read lessons, and only with a local model; chat recall passed include_lessons: false, so the 255 public copies never reached a conversation. Restoring therefore includes building the first chat reader.

Design changes from the live-data review (n-agli-ev-lessons-widen-20261123):
1. Auto-pass only on an allowlist: group-sourced, low-sensitivity lessons that stand on their own without the original event. Always send to the owner: private-chat sources; identifiable health, relationship, family, finance or workplace-conflict content; group nicknames or jokes; unpublished research; anything that guesses someone's wishes. Stop lessons that encourage deception, mockery or bypassing permissions at the private stage.
2. Make the judgement checkable. Verify answers three questions: which two clues would let an acquaintance recognize the source; what the original teller might mind; whether it labels people or encourages misconduct. The review obeys processing policy instead of ProcessingPolicy::Any. Record forwarded, call failure, unparseable output and verdict/reason mismatch separately, with the raw output. For each new model or prompt version, compare 50 items by hand, then sample 20% of auto-passes. These are starting values, not proven thresholds.
3. Make copies revocable before any chat reader. A pass creates one derived item bound to source, text version, audience and processing policy; a rewrite approves only the new text; revocation also clears search indexes and session caches.

Acceptance: a synthetic private chat yields a private lesson; after review only the public copy enters a group-chat prompt; restricted source text, review reasons and revoked copies never enter that prompt. Cover a processor change, a restart reload and the no-reviewer case.

Existing data: keep the lessons collection. 48 of the 250 auto-passed public copies should not stay public as written (7 retract, 41 rewrite); retire them before the chat reader goes live. Per-item list: data/reviews/2026-11-23-lessons-widen.md (gitignored).
