Image bytes reach disk before captioning creates a record. The age-based sweep enumerates records, leaving unrecorded files outside the retention window.

Acceptance
Drive budget denial, caption failure and interruption after the byte write. Advance a fixture clock past retention. Expired, unreferenced bytes disappear and retained assets sharing the bytes remain readable across restart.
