ControlPlane::load treats a read error or a wrong type as not frozen, so a frozen instance can resume proactive work after a restart. /freeze also holds the admission lock and must write Mongo before it closes admission and cancels work.

Keep the bot disabled, or abort startup, when the flag cannot be read. On /freeze, close local admission and cancel first, then report persistence and termination separately.
